How it works:
- While the POS runs, it redraws one phone-sized picture, canli_durum.png, every 30 seconds.
- It goes into a pos-monitor folder in that PC's Google Drive, created automatically and found the same way as the backups: G:\Drive'ım\pos-monitor\ on Turkish PCs, G:\My Drive\pos-monitor\ on yours.
- It overwrites the same file each time, so the Drive link stays the same and old pictures never pile up. Stray copies Drive sometimes makes (e.g. canli_durum (1).png) are deleted automatically.

What the picture shows (preview at C:\Users\hikme\AppData\Local\Temp\claude\31595605043e0450\94be29bb-7ee3-4a10-b4b8-b2d47faf46e0\scratchpad\shots\monitor_fixed.png):
- open table count, open total, today's revenue, and today's cancellations (count and ₺);
- every open table, longest first, with its area, how long it's been open ("8 dk", "1 sa 15 dk", "1 gün 2 sa"), its total or remaining amount if part-paid, and a "QR bekliyor" tag;
- today's cancellations: time, table, product, quantity, reason and amount;
- the last-updated time at the top, so you can tell if the POS has stopped.

How you view it: open the Dr

Important for client cafés: the picture lands in the Google account signed in on that café's PC. That's also why I didn't use your folder link: its ID belongs to your account and doesn't exist on a client's PC. To see it from your own phone, share their pos-monitor folder with your Google account once in Drive, or sign the café PC in with your account.

Testing:
- The test server wrote the picture straight away, rewrote it after 30 seconds, and deleted a planted stray copy. The numbers matched the test data.
- I fixed three things in the picture: a missing clock symbol in the font, "BUGÜN İPTAL" spelling, and day-length durations.
- The build self-test now draws the picture too, so a missing library would stop the installer build.
- All 8 browser suites still pass.
- Nothing was written to your real Drive. On this PC the target resolves to G:\My Drive\pos-monitor, but that folder only appears once a real POS with this version runs.

Settings in config.py: MONITOR_ENABLED, MONITOR_EVERY_SEC (30), MONITOR_FOLDER_NAME. The POS_MONITOR_DIR setting can send it to another folder, or turn it off if left empty. Drive keeps older versions of the overwritten file for a while (up to 100, about 64 KB each), which is negligible.
